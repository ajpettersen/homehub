---
name: Maintenance one-time scheduling
description: Defines the expected lifecycle for date-specific maintenance reminders.
---

One-time maintenance reminders use a specific due date, do not have a recurrence interval, and leave the active maintenance lists after completion. Recurring tasks retain their existing rescheduling behavior.

**Why:** A calendar appointment or an isolated repair should not be pushed forward as though it were a periodic home-care routine.

**How to apply:** When adding maintenance surfaces, show the distinction between one-time and recurring schedules, request a due date for one-time reminders, and exclude completed one-time reminders from active counts and upcoming lists.
